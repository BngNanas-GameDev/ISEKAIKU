Asimov adds new characters (Roman numerals, Russian Ruble sign, interrobang,
triple prime) and additional widths/styles, and modifies a few glyphs
(number 1 and 4, quotation mark, dollar sign, apostrophe, ampersand).

Font is used unmodified in this application.

Source: https://www.fontspace.com/asimov-font-f22365
